# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day – 09/05/2026”

### Event Objectives

- Share a "brain hacking" method to turn learning into an addictive habit.
- Share Automated Prompt Engineering techniques and introduce an automated prompt-optimization solution (Proptimizer) on AWS.
- Help young professionals position themselves and adapt their mindset in the AI era.
- Introduce the BMAD Method to help control source code quality and reduce AI hallucination.

### Speakers

- **Huynh Hoang Long** – Admin, FCAJ
- **Nguyen Tuan Thinh** – DevOps/Cloud Engineer
- **Anh Khang** – Solution Architect at Cloud Kinetics
- **Chi Thao** – Software Developer at Vietnam International Bank (VIB)

### Key Highlights

#### The Dopamine Hack – Turning learning into an addictive habit

- **Applying Fear of Loss:** Building a daily streak/check-in system to create study pressure.
- **Tricking the Amygdala:** Breaking large goals into smaller ones so the brain isn't overwhelmed, and applying the 2-minute rule (if it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now — don't procrastinate).
- **Creating Instant Feedback:** Building a leveling system based on accumulated knowledge, where reaching a certain quota earns a reward (based on how Dopamine works).

#### Automated Prompt Engineering technique

- A good prompt framework needs 7 core components: Role, Instruction, Context, Input, Output Format, Examples, Constraints.

#### Positioning yourself in the AI era

- **AI Amplify:** AI only amplifies your ability (if you're weak, it makes you weaker; if you're strong, it makes you stronger).
- **Integrity:** Play the long game — proactively dig deep and resolve edge cases.
- **Hiring criteria:** Attitude - Skill Level - Experience / Exposure – Aptitude.

#### The BMAD Method

- Shifting from code management to document management (document-first).
- **Multi-Agent workflow:** Breaking a project down through independent agents (PM → Architect → Sharding → Scrum Master → Dev → QA) to avoid context overflow and reduce generated junk code.

### Key Takeaways

#### Growth Mindset

- Learned additional ways to sustain learning through the "Dopamine Hack" mechanism.
- Broadened perspective: job benefits aren't just financial (salary) — there are 4 other key factors (experience, network, knowledge, and exposure).

#### Technical Architecture

- Breaking large problems into smaller ones so AI can execute them accurately.
- Gained a practical understanding of how AWS services operate through a real application (Proptimizer).

### Applying to Work

- **Standardizing prompts:** Writing structured code-generation prompts with tight constraints to avoid wasting tokens.
- **Applying the BMAD mindset:** Designing clear system and feature documentation before handing tasks to AI for coding.

### Event Experience

Attending the Meetup was a very valuable experience — it provided a lot of useful information on career direction while introducing new methods for mastering AI. Some highlights:

#### Learning from highly experienced speakers

- The speaker from **Cloud Kinetics** gave a candid perspective on today's hiring market.
- I gained a better understanding of what criteria are used to evaluate a fresher candidate, beyond just grades.

#### Hands-on with visual tools

- Watched a live demo of **"Proptimizer"** - an application dedicated to optimizing prompt statements, which helped visualize how the product actually runs on AWS.
- Explored the BMAD Method's use of independent Multi-Agent models to narrow the context window during software development, improving AI control and reducing "junk code" or hallucination.

#### Lessons Learned

- The "Dopamine Hack" learning strategy increases learning effectiveness.
- Programming in the AI era needs a systematic approach: tasks must be broken down for specialized AI agents to execute, instead of "coding carelessly."
