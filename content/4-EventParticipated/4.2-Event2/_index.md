# Event Report: “Meet-up with First Cloud AI Journey Members and Personal Development Skills”

### Event Objectives

- Share knowledge to improve technology skills
- Share hands-on experience from HACKATHON
- Share soft skills
- Introduce the fortune-telling website

### Speakers

- **Huynh Thai Linh** – Advanced AWS Skills with Cloud Quest and Floci
- **Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan** – HACKATHON
- **Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu** – Why We Need to Be Confident
- **Tran Nghia** – Đại Việt Fortune-Telling
- **Tran Minh Quan** – The Hidden Iceberg of a Project: DevOps Before Disaster
- **Pham Khac Huy - The Iceberg of Procrastination**

### Key Highlights

#### Advanced AWS Skills with Cloud Quest and Floci

- Introduced an effective learning path through AWS Cloud Quest and the Floci solution, which offers clear advantages over LocalStack for optimizing testing environments and simulating the cloud.

#### HACKATHON

- Provided a practical view of the intense Hackathon environment, how to build solutions quickly, and the painful lessons learned from solving real-world problems.

#### Why We Need to Be Confident

- Analyzed the costs of lacking confidence and self-doubt, while emphasizing the core value of vulnerability as a source of strength that helps connect people.

#### Đại Việt Fortune-Telling

- Offered a unique perspective by combining traditional culture to decode destiny and better understand oneself in a scientific way.

#### The Hidden Iceberg of a Project: DevOps Before Disaster

- Discussed the hidden risks behind a seemingly simple feature, ambiguous system requirements, and the critical importance of documentation and DevOps processes.

#### The Iceberg of Procrastination

- Explored the psychology behind procrastination and pointed out its underlying causes.

### What I Learned

- **Technology**: Gained a better understanding of how to move infrastructure to the cloud, how to use free playgrounds to practice AWS safely without worrying about unexpected costs, and became aware of the hidden costs of system errors in DevOps.

- **Mindset and psychology**: Recognized that procrastination is not simply laziness, but a sign of emotional imbalance and difficulty in self-regulation.

### Event Experience

- **High interactivity**: Participants were strongly inspired through real stories and relatable examples.

#### Key Lessons Learned

- Large problems related to procrastination often accumulate from deep hidden causes. We should proactively dig to the root cause instead of only treating the surface symptoms.

- Build confidence in front of a crowd or in front of other people.
