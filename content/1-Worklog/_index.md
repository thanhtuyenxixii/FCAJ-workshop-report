**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? **How many weeks** did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [AWS Free Tier setup, AWS Console basics, and cost management](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Amazon EC2 hands-on and Amazon RDS database fundamentals](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Server and database migration, backup, and hybrid DNS](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Networking with VPC Peering, Transit Gateway, and resource tagging](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Infrastructure as Code, serverless automation, and SSO](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Cloud security, containerization with Docker/ECS, and CI/CD](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Data Lake, hybrid storage, and cloud cost optimization](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Cost analysis and application modernization](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Serverless workflows and event-driven microservices](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Amazon EKS, CI/CD pipelines, and serverless frontend](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Serverless apps, PaaS deployment, and container hosting](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12:** [Security, data lake, BI, and machine learning wrap-up](1.12-week12/)
